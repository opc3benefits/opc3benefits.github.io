Tip for building the habit: keep a small ‘hydration station’ at home—a measuring cup, clean bottle, and a dry scoop. A tiny setup cuts friction and removes guesswork. If you travel, a collapsible cup and a labeled baggie keep things tidy.

Finally, track what you actually do rather than what you intend. A simple spreadsheet or notebook with columns for date, mixing time, water amount, and notes will reveal patterns in a week or two. Use that feedback to adjust the time of day or prep steps.
